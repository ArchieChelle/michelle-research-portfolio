# [Post Title]

> **The promise (one sentence):** What will the reader walk away knowing or believing? If you can't write this in one line, the post isn't ready.

---

## Hook

The opening that earns the next paragraph. Three options:

- **Tension:** Name a contradiction the reader feels but hasn't named.
- **Story:** A 2-sentence scene that makes the abstract concrete.
- **Counter-take:** Push against conventional wisdom in the field.

## The argument

What are you actually saying? Make the claim clearly, then defend it.

- Claim
- Why most people get it wrong
- What the better frame is

## Evidence

What backs the argument? Pull from your case studies, from research, from the field. Be specific. Numbers earn trust.

## So what

Why should the reader care today? What can they do with this?

## Close

Land the plane. End on a sentence the reader could quote.
