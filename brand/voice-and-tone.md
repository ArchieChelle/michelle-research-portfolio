# Voice & Tone

The michelle.research voice is **polished, formal, confident** — but never stuffy.

## Core traits

| Trait | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Strategic** | Frame work in business terms, not method jargon. The reader should see impact before they see methodology. |
| **Confident** | No hedging. No "I think" or "I tried to." State what was done and what changed. |
| **Specific** | Numbers earn trust. `26% improvement` beats `significant improvement` every time. |
| **Editorial** | Think New Yorker, not LinkedIn. Sentences breathe. White space matters. |
| **Generous** | Credit cross-functional partners. Research that moves teams is research that mattered. |

## We sound like / we don't sound like

| We sound like | We don't sound like |
|---|---|
| Direct, considered, generous with context. | Salesy, breathless, or buzzword-heavy. |
| "Here's what we found, and what we'd do next." | "Synergizing user-centric paradigms." |
| Confident with our limits — clear about what we don't yet know. | Falsely certain or apologetically vague. |
| Numbers that earn trust. `26% improvement` beats `significant improvement`. | Vague superlatives with no evidence. |

## Sentence-level rules

- **No filler.** "In order to" → "to". "Due to the fact that" → "because". Cut.
- **Active voice.** "I led" beats "research was led by me."
- **Lead with the outcome.** Results-first sentences land harder than narrative-first ones.
- **One claim per sentence.** Don't stack hedges.

## Phrases to avoid

| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "I had the opportunity to..." | "I led..." |
| "Helped to deliver..." | "Delivered..." |
| "Worked closely with..." | "Partnered with..." |
| "Significant impact" | (give a number) |
| "Cutting-edge" | (describe what it actually does) |
| "Passionate about UX" | (show it through the work) |

## Tone calibration by surface

- **Hero copy:** Bold, direct, almost manifesto-like
- **Case studies:** Editorial — set the scene, then dig in
- **Project cards:** Tight — one outcome line, that's it
- **Writing posts:** Conversational with strategic backbone
- **Contact:** Warm but professional — "Let's build what's next"

## The test

Read it out loud. If it sounds like a job applicant, rewrite it. If it sounds like a strategist, ship it.
