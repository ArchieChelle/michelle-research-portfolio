# Typography

Quick reference for Astro text styles.

## Font families

| Use | Font | Weights | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display & all headings | **Newsreader** | 300–800, italic | Google Fonts |
| Body, UI & navigation | **IBM Plex Serif** | 300, 400, 500, 600 | Google Fonts |
| Logo wordmark (SVG only) | **IBM Plex Serif SemiBold** | 600 | Google Fonts |

**Note:** IBM Plex Serif SemiBold (600) is reserved exclusively for the logo wordmark SVG. It is not used for editorial headings or body copy.

## Type scale

| Role | Font | Size | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 / Display | Newsreader | 68px | 1.05 |
| H2 | Newsreader | 42px | 1.10 |
| H3 | Newsreader | 28px | 1.20 |
| Body | IBM Plex Serif | 18px | 1.55 |
| Small / Labels | IBM Plex Serif | 14px | 1.50 |

## Pairing rules

- **Newsreader** is the editorial voice — use it for everything readers should slow down for
- **IBM Plex Serif** is the working voice — use it for everything readers should scan: body copy, nav, labels, buttons, captions
- Italic in Newsreader carries the brand's literary cadence — use it for pull-quotes, section title emphasis, and hero statements

## Don't

- Don't use IBM Plex Sans — the body font is IBM Plex Serif
- Don't use Newsreader for body copy (exhausting at 18px in continuous text)
- Don't use IBM Plex Serif SemiBold for editorial headings — SemiBold is the logo wordmark's exclusive weight
- Don't introduce a third display font
