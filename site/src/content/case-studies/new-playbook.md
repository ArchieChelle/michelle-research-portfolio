role: Lead UX Researcher
sample: 18 internal stakeholder interviews and Kano survey n=201
team: Product, Engineering, Analytics, UX, Senior Leadership
challenge:
  - Thirteen concepts were competing for mobile roadmap investment with no shared decision frame.
  - Leadership needed evidence they could trust across user value, feasibility, and strategic fit.
approach:
  - Conducted 18 stakeholder interviews, synthesized themes, and facilitated a cross-functional vision workshop.
  - Ran a Kano survey with 201 participants and ranked concepts by self-stated importance when Kano classification came back near-uniform.
findings:
  - The roadmap needed shared decision criteria before more ideation.
  - Workshop outputs created traceability from strategic themes to testable feature concepts.
  - Prioritization logic narrowed 13 concepts to 5 investments using multiple criteria.
dataAnalysis:
  - Kano classification (continuous analysis) placed 12 of 13 concepts as Attractive, leaving importance score as the differentiator.
  - Importance ranking (3.38–5.68) set the cut line, weighed against the existing 2023 roadmap sequence.
impact:
  - Concentrated 2023 mobile engineering capacity on 5 funded investments instead of a 13-item wishlist.
  - Deferred 8 lower-importance concepts, giving Product and Engineering a focused execution list.
