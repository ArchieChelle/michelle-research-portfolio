role: Lead UX Researcher
sample: 18 executive stakeholder interviews and Kano survey n=201
team: Product, Engineering, Analytics, UX, Senior Leadership
challenge:
  - Thirteen concepts were competing for mobile roadmap investment with no shared decision frame.
  - Leadership needed evidence they could trust across user value, feasibility, and strategic fit.
approach:
  - Conducted 18 stakeholder interviews, synthesized themes, and facilitated a cross-functional vision workshop.
  - Ran a Kano survey with 201 participants and combined satisfaction signals with feasibility and strategic fit.
findings:
  - The roadmap needed shared decision criteria before more ideation.
  - Workshop outputs created traceability from strategic themes to testable feature concepts.
  - Prioritization logic narrowed 13 concepts to 5 investments using multiple criteria.
dataAnalysis:
  - Kano classification informed concept satisfaction impact.
  - Final matrix connected Kano result, user priority, feasibility, and strategic fit.
impact:
  - Leadership gained a clearer evidence base for investment sequencing.
  - Eight concepts were deprioritized to focus execution on five strategic bets.
