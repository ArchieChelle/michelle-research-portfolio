  - Evaluative Research
  - Feature Usefulness Survey
  - Tier-Fit Analysis

researchApproach: Quantitative
methodologyTags:
  - Feature Usefulness Survey
  - Tier-Fit Analysis
role: UX Researcher
sample: n=250 SE Play users via in-product Qualtrics intercept
team: Sales Director, Price Valuation Team, Product, Research
challenge:
  - Team needed to map valued features to the right subscription tiers without overstating pricing evidence.
  - Signals like usefulness, willingness to pay, and churn were related but not equivalent.
approach:
  - Measured perceived feature usefulness and mapped values to current tier packaging.
  - Sequenced follow-up research across churn surveys, interviews, cohort analysis, and willingness-to-pay validation.
findings:
  - Tier value was a fit problem before a pricing problem.
  - All-Access value concentrated around access and flexible viewing features.
  - Downgrade risk remained a hypothesis pending behavioral churn validation.
dataAnalysis:
  - Feature usefulness and tier-fit evidence informed packaging recommendations.
  - "Evidence boundary: study did not directly measure willingness to pay or prove churn causality."
impact:
  - Pricing conversations shifted from broad strategy to feature-tier alignment.
  - Follow-up research roadmap defined the path from attitudes to behavior and pricing sensitivity.
