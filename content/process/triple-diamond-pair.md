## Framework: PAIR (People + AI Research)

PAIR governs how AI shows up at each stage of the work — and where human judgment stays in the lead.

### Pre-study
**AI assists with:**
- Desk research synthesis
- Screener generation
- Discussion guide drafting

**Human leads:** research questions, hypothesis framing, study design rigor.

### During study
**AI assists with:**
- Live transcription
- Sentiment flagging
- Real-time note structuring

**Human leads:** rapport, probing, reading the room, ethical judgment in the moment.

### Post-study
**AI assists with:**
- Synthesis acceleration
- Tagging and clustering
- Reporting drafts

**Human leads:** **all decisions.** Insight calls, recommendation framing, stakeholder narrative.
